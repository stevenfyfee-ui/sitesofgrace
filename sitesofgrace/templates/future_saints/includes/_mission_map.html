{% load static %} {% comment %} The illustrated California map. Two layers in one fixed viewBox, inside a zoomable stage: 1. .fs-map-art (aria-hidden): Steven's watercolor textures, the land one clipped to the real outline (Census, public domain; projected by future_saints.geo) and the ocean one to the Pacific; the neighbouring states as plain parchment; his ship and whale in open ocean (missions.CALIFORNIA_ART); the dotted trail in the Mission Trail's stop order; four city ribbons with leader lines to each city's real position; the compass rose and scroll. 2. .fs-map-pins: the interactive layer. Each mission is a real submit button of a GET form (so it works without JavaScript), at its canonical latitude/longitude through the same projection, in founding order (= tab order). The box keeps the viewBox's aspect ratio, so percentages match the art. Zoom (future-saints.js): 1x-4x, +/-/reset, drag and pinch, the wheel only once the map is clicked or focused; pins and badges keep their size. Without JavaScript nothing zooms. {% endcomment %}
