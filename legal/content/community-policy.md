# Community, Photos & User Content Policy

Sites of Grace invites pilgrims to keep track of places they have visited and, where enabled, to share photos, corrections, and personal experiences. These guidelines protect the dignity, privacy, and rights of contributors and of the people who appear in what they share. They supplement our [Terms of Use](/terms-of-use/) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).

## 1. Share only what you have the right to share
You must own the material or have every permission needed to upload and publish it, including the rights of photographers, artists, publishers, and identifiable people in it. Do not upload copyrighted photos from shrine websites, travel companies, newspapers, or social networks unless a license or other lawful basis allows it. Adding a photo credit alone does not give you permission.

## 2. Respect people and sacred places
Submissions should relate to pilgrimage, sacred sites, faith, or travel. Do not post harassment, hate, threats, misleading impersonation, solicitation, unlawful content, malware, or anything that violates someone's privacy. Be respectful of worship, local customs, clergy, pilgrims, and people who are ill or grieving.

## 3. Who can share publicly, and photos of other people
You must be 18 or older to share photos publicly or make your profile public. Do not publish a photo of a child unless the child is your own or you have the legal permission of the child's parent or guardian. Do not include the names, schools, home addresses, precise locations, or medical details of minors. Ask permission before sharing recognizable photos of other adults, especially in sensitive settings.

## 4. Health, prayer intentions, and personal information
Do not publish detailed medical or other sensitive personal records about yourself or anyone else. Stories of healing should be shared as personal experiences, not as established medical or scientific claims. Private prayer intentions, sacramental information, and confidential conversations should stay private unless everyone involved has agreed.

## 5. Public visibility and location information
Your profile is private by default, and your photo library stays private unless you share a photo. Followers you approve can see your Visited and Want-to-Go lists; if you make your profile public, anyone can. Once you share a photo or story publicly, anyone can see it, and it may be found in search engines, copied, or shared by others. Photos can carry hidden location data from your camera or phone, so check before you share. You can stop sharing a photo at any time from your photo library. You can also ask us to remove something by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, but copies others have made may remain outside our control.

## 6. License to display your content
You keep ownership of the rights you hold in your material. To make publishing possible, you grant Sites of Grace a nonexclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to store, resize, format, display, and distribute your submission on the Site. We will not use your submission in advertising, printed materials, or our social media accounts without asking you first. You confirm that you are entitled to grant this license.

## 7. Moderation and reporting
We may review, decline, format, hide, or remove submissions that break these rules or raise legal concerns. We do not promise to review every submission before it is published. To report a privacy concern, copyright problem, or other inappropriate material, send the page link and a short description to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Copyright owners should also see our [Copyright & DMCA Policy](/copyright-policy/).

## 8. Account eligibility and enforcement
Account holders must meet the age rules in our [Terms of Use](/terms-of-use/). Repeated violations may lead to loss of sharing privileges, removal of material, or account suspension, consistent with applicable law. We may update these guidelines as community features change.
