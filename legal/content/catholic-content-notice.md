# Catholic Content, Church Status & Testimony Notice

## Independent Catholic resource
Sites of Grace is an independently operated Catholic educational and pilgrimage website. Unless expressly stated, it is not an official publication or agency of the Holy See, a diocese, parish, religious community, apparition site, shrine, or pilgrimage organization, and it has not been endorsed by them. Our purpose is to make Catholic history, holy places, and pilgrimage resources easier to explore.

## Apparitions, Eucharistic miracles, and Church recognition
The Church's assessment of reported apparitions, miracles, and devotional practices can vary by place and can change over time. We try to distinguish official decisions, permitted devotion, historical traditions, and unverified reports. Describing a reported event is not a statement that the Catholic Church has confirmed its supernatural origin. For a definitive Church status, consult current statements by the competent bishop, diocese, or the Holy See.

## Personal stories and reported healings
Pilgrim stories and accounts of favors or healings are personal reports, shared for historical, religious, or informational context. Unless specifically stated and supported by an identified official process, including a report does not imply independent medical verification or Church recognition. No spiritual practice, shrine visit, relic, or devotional item is presented as a promised cure or guaranteed result. Seek professional medical advice for medical concerns, and do not delay recommended treatment because of anything on this site.

## Teaching and religious guidance
Educational articles are general explanations and do not replace Church teaching, pastoral care, or individual spiritual direction. For questions about doctrine, the sacraments, or your own spiritual situation, consult the Catechism, official Church sources, and a priest or qualified pastoral adviser.
