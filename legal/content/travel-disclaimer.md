# Travel & Pilgrimage Disclaimer

Sites of Grace provides educational information and general tools to help individuals research Catholic pilgrimage destinations. Content on this website, including guides, itineraries, maps, budget estimates, suggested hotels, and tour referrals, is not a personalized travel service or a guarantee of any particular experience.

## Verify important details before traveling
Schedules, access rules, services, ticket policies, Mass and confession times, visas, entry requirements, transportation routes, local security conditions, accessibility services, and prices can change without notice. Always confirm current details directly with official shrine or parish contacts, transportation providers, accommodations, and the relevant government authorities before making purchases or setting out.

## Trip budgets and estimates
Cost calculators and examples are illustrative estimates only, not live quotes, guaranteed prices, or binding offers. Actual charges can differ substantially based on dates, location, taxes, luggage, group size, exchange rates, availability, insurance, and service fees. Verify final prices and what is included with the actual seller before booking.

## Tour operators, hotels, and referral links
When we link to tour groups, pilgrimage organizations, hotels, or booking platforms, we are presenting independent third-party options. Unless expressly stated otherwise, Sites of Grace does not itself arrange, sell, operate, supervise, or insure these trips and accommodations. We may receive referral compensation, which is disclosed near the affected recommendations. Third parties are responsible for their own booking terms, payment collection, safety practices, services, cancellations, and refunds.

## Maps and accessibility
Map markers, walking times, hotel distances, and route suggestions are approximate and may not account for road closures, steep grades, stairs, reduced-mobility access, construction, or other conditions. Travelers with accessibility, medical, or mobility needs should get written confirmation directly from facilities and providers. Do not rely only on a map for safety-critical decisions.

## Safety, health, and personal judgment
Travelers are responsible for their own decisions about insurance, travel documents, health precautions, personal safety, and emergency arrangements. If you have a health condition or disability, talk with qualified professionals about your travel needs; prayer and pilgrimage are not substitutes for medical care. Check government travel advisories and other official sources for your destination.
