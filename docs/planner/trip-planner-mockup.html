Build your pilgrimage

Choose sacred places, tell us about the trip, and get a day-by-day plan with an estimated budget. Save it, print it, or send it to the people you're traveling with.

✦ Added from Our Lady of Lourdes
Replaces today's cost estimator at the same URL. "Add to a trip plan" on a site page links to /plan/?site=<slug>, adds that site's destination as the first stop and shows this chip. The old estimator's math becomes the budget card on the right.

Step 1Where do you want to go?

Add up to six stops in the order you'll visit them. You can search for a town or a sacred site.

Popular:
    Add a place to start your plan.
    Search covers the planner destinations (today's 22 cost records) and every live sacred site. Picking a site adds its destination (via the existing associated_sites link or locality match). A site with no destination shows "We can't plan around this one yet" and a link to its page. Near your route suggests the closest other destination within about 150 miles of a stop. Nights default to the curated plan's length.

    Step 2About the trip

    Used for dates, the budget, and how full each day is.

    Flight estimates are round trips from the U.S. by region (the existing RegionFlightAssumption records), so "Flying from" is a single option for now. Pace only changes automatically grouped days (2, 3 or 4 sites a day). Curated days stay as written.

    Step 3Your day-by-day plan

    ✓ Curated plan written by Sites of Grace Suggested order grouped from the destination's sacred sites
    Each destination gets an optional curated plan: a few day rows (a title plus linked sacred sites), written by you in Wagtail. We start with the 22 planner destinations, flagship ones first. Destinations without one fall back to Suggested order: their live sites in nearest-neighbor order, split by pace. The first day is always a light arrival day. Extra nights become free days. Curated days that don't fit are shown faded with "add a night". Between stops we show distance in whole miles only, no drive times, matching the Nearby decision. Mass times are never listed; each site links to its page.
