## Reporting a copyright concern
If you believe material on SitesOfGrace.com infringes your copyright, please send a written notice to our designated copyright agent. To be effective under 17 U.S.C. 512(c)(3), your notice should include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature as the rights owner or authorized representative.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list of works if several are involved.
3. Identification of the material you believe is infringing, with enough detail, including its URL, for us to find it.
4. Your name and contact information, such as email address, mailing address, and phone number.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the rights owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the rights owner.

## Counter-notification
If your material was removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe the removal was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notification that includes the information required by 17 U.S.C. 512(g): your signature; identification of the removed material and where it appeared; a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your name, address, and telephone number; and your consent to the required jurisdiction and service of process. Counter-notices can have legal consequences, so consider getting legal advice before sending one. We will handle valid counter-notices as the law requires.
