## 2. Information we collect
Depending on how you use the site, we may collect the following:

- **Account information:** your email address, your password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), and your confirmation that you are at least 13. We create a username for you automatically. If you choose, you can add a profile photo, a short bio, and your home city and country. We never ask for your street address.
- **Pilgrimage and community information:** places you save or mark as visited, photos, captions, stories, corrections, and other content you submit. Saved religious destinations may indirectly reveal your religious interests, so please share only what you are comfortable providing.
- **Communications:** what you send us by email, and the email address you give when you sign up for our newsletter or join a product waitlist.
- **Payment information:** support payments are made on Stripe's secure checkout page. Your card details go directly to Stripe; our website never receives or stores them. Stripe shares with us the amount, date, payment status, and the name and email address you give at checkout.
- **Technical information:** like most websites, our hosting provider records basic request information, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to run and protect the site. We also use IP addresses briefly to limit abuse, such as repeated sign-in attempts or form spam. We do not use analytics or advertising tools on our own pages.
- **Information from other companies:** we do not buy personal information or receive it from other companies, apart from the payment details Stripe shares with us. We do not offer sign-in through other services.

Please do not include medical records, diagnoses, the locations of children, or other highly sensitive information in a public story or photo caption.

## 3. How we use information
We use information as reasonably needed to provide and secure the website; create and manage accounts; save your pilgrimage journeys; display content you choose to share; respond to messages; send newsletters you ask for; process support payments and product waitlists; investigate misuse; meet legal obligations; and improve our educational and travel-planning resources. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

## 4. When information is public
Your profile is private by default. On a private profile, anyone can see your profile photo, display name, and username, so people can find you and ask to follow you. Only followers you approve can see the rest of your profile, including your badges, follower counts, and Visited and Want-to-Go lists. If you make your profile public, anyone can see them, and anyone can follow you without needing your approval. Your photo library stays private unless you share a photo. A photo you share to a sacred site's page can be seen by anyone, along with its caption and the name you choose for credit. Profile photos are stored so they can be shown on the site, and anyone with an image's link can view it. We remove the location data that cameras and phones can embed in photo files when you upload them. Do not post private details or photos of someone else without their permission.

## 5. Who we share information with
We share personal information only with companies that help us run the site, and only as much as each one needs:

- **DigitalOcean** hosts the website, its database, and uploaded photos, in the United States.
- **Resend** sends account, password-reset, and site emails.
- **Stripe** processes support payments on its own checkout and customer-portal pages.
- **Google Fonts** supplies the site's typefaces. Your browser connects to Google to load them, so Google receives your IP address.
- **Esri** supplies the map images on our interactive maps. Your browser connects to Esri to load them, so Esri receives your IP address.
- **Wikimedia Commons** supplies many saint portraits and historical images. Your browser loads them from Wikimedia's servers, so Wikimedia receives your IP address.
- **News publishers:** the images in our home page news feed load directly from the publishers' own image servers, which receive your IP address.
- **Video providers:** if an article includes an embedded video, such as from YouTube or Vimeo, that provider receives your IP address and may set its own cookies when the video loads.
- **Amazon** (through the Amazon Associates program) receives your visit when you follow one of our book or product links, and sets its own cookies.

If you follow a link to a hotel, tour operator, retailer, or social media site, that company controls the information it collects on its own site.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not run advertising on the site. Affiliate partners may use cookies to credit us when you buy or book something after following one of our links, as described in section 6. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights and safety of others, or as part of a transfer of the website to a new owner.

## 6. Cookies, similar technologies, and browser signals
We use a small number of cookies that the site needs in order to work: to keep you signed in, protect forms from forgery, and show one-time messages. Our [Cookies & Tracking Notice](/cookie-notice/) lists them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but signing in will not work without them.

**Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control:** we do not run analytics or advertising trackers on our own pages, and we do not sell personal information, so the site works the same way whether or not your browser sends one of these signals.

**Third-party tracking:** when you follow an affiliate link or play an embedded video, those companies may collect information about your activity over time and across different websites under their own privacy policies.

## 7. Email, newsletter, and waitlists
If you sign up for our newsletter, we use your email address to send it. Joining a product waitlist gets you updates about that product only; it does not sign you up for the newsletter. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. We may still send account, payment, and security emails after you unsubscribe from marketing.

## 8. How long we keep information
- **Account information, saved places, and your photo library:** while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and its content within 30 days.
- **Photos and stories you share publicly:** until you remove them, delete your account, or we remove them under our [Community Policy](/community-policy/).
- **Newsletter signup:** until you unsubscribe. After that we keep only your email address on a do-not-send list, so we do not email you again by mistake.
- **Messages you send us:** as long as needed to respond and keep a reasonable record of the conversation.
- **Payment records:** Stripe holds your payment records. We keep the records we need for tax and accounting.
- **Backups:** deleted information can remain in backup copies for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no website or method of transmission can guarantee complete security.
