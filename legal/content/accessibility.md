# Accessibility Statement

Sites of Grace aims to make its sacred-site information, educational articles, and pilgrimage-planning tools usable by as many people as possible, including visitors who use assistive technologies.

We are working to improve accessibility by using clear text, descriptive links, structured headings, keyboard-operable features, text descriptions for meaningful images, and accessible forms and navigation.

If you run into a barrier while using our maps, trip calculator, account features, photos, or any other part of the site, please contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Include the page address, the problem, and how you would like us to reply. We will make reasonable efforts to help you get the information or feature you need and to improve that part of the site.

Some content comes from outside providers whose accessibility practices are beyond our direct control. We welcome feedback and will keep reviewing accessibility as the site grows.
