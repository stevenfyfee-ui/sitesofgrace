{% comment %} The world map. Two layers in one fixed Equal Earth viewBox, inside a zoomable stage: 1. .fs-map-art (aria-hidden): _world_art.html (textures, outline, ocean-only art, compass). No country labels. 2. .fs-wmap-pins: one marker per pinned story, a real submit button of a GET form (?story=), at its true spot (future_saints.worldmap); data-x/y are its map units. On desktop near 1x a reviewed offset may move a marker clear of its neighbours, with a thin line to its spot; zooming past 2x fades the offsets out (the pins separate). Zoom (future-saints.js): 1x-6x, region shortcuts, +/-/reset, drag and pinch, the wheel only once the map is clicked or focused; markers keep their size. Without JavaScript nothing zooms and the shortcuts and controls stay hidden. {% endcomment %}
