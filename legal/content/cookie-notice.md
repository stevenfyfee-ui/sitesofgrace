# Cookies & Tracking Notice

This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies used on Sites of Grace and by the outside services our pages connect to. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Cookies the site needs to work
We set only the cookies the site needs to work. We do not use them for analytics or advertising.

- **sessionid**: keeps you signed in to your pilgrim account; expires when you close your browser, or after two weeks if you choose "Remember me" when you sign in.
- **csrftoken**: form security; protects the forms you submit from being sent by another website; expires after one year.
- **messages**: shows a one-time message, such as a sign-in confirmation, on the next page you see; deleted as soon as the message is shown, or when you close your browser.

The site also saves one setting in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie:

- **sog-map-legend-collapsed**: remembers whether you closed the legend on the interactive map; stays until you clear your browser's site data.

## Analytics
We do not use analytics cookies or analytics tools.

## Affiliate tracking and outside services
- **Amazon:** when you follow one of our book or product links, Amazon sets its own cookies to credit qualifying purchases to us. Other affiliate partners we link to may do the same.
- **Stripe:** support payments and monthly-support management happen on Stripe's own checkout and customer-portal pages, which use Stripe's cookies for security and fraud prevention.
- **Embedded videos:** if an article includes a video from a provider such as YouTube or Vimeo, that provider may set its own cookies when the video loads.
- **Images and fonts from other servers:** your browser connects to Google to load the site's typefaces, to Esri to load map images, to Wikimedia Commons for many saint portraits, and to news publishers' servers for news-feed images. These services receive your IP address and browser information when they load.

These companies handle what they collect under their own privacy policies.

## Your choices
You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the cookies the site needs, signing in and submitting forms will not work. Because we do not use optional cookies of our own, the site has no cookie banner or settings panel. If that changes, we will add controls and update this notice.
