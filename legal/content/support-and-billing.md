# Support Payments & Recurring Billing Notice

## What support means
Sites of Grace is independently operated and offers its educational and pilgrimage resources for free. If you choose to support the site, your payment helps cover ordinary operating costs such as research, hosting, photography, map development, and maintenance. Support is entirely optional. It does not unlock special features or guarantee any particular new content.

## Not a tax-deductible charitable contribution
Sites of Grace is not a tax-exempt charitable organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. Support payments are not tax-deductible charitable contributions.

## One-time and monthly support
You can give once or monthly, in U.S. dollars, in any amount within the range shown on our Support page. Before you pay, Stripe's checkout page shows the amount and whether the charge is one-time or monthly. Monthly support is charged at the amount you chose each month, starting on the day you sign up, until you cancel. We will not change the amount of an existing monthly gift; to give a different amount, cancel and start new support at the amount you want. Stripe emails you a receipt for each payment.

## Payment processing
Payments are processed by Stripe on its secure checkout page. Please review Stripe's checkout terms and privacy policy. Our website never receives or stores your card number. Stripe shares with us the amount, date, payment status, and the name and email address you give at checkout.

## Change or cancel monthly support
Use **Manage monthly support** on our [Support page](/support/), which opens Stripe's secure customer portal, or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. When you cancel, no further charges are made. Your current month, which you have already paid for, is not refunded.

## Refunds and payment questions
If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or did not authorize a payment, contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} within 60 days and we will refund it. For any other refund request, write to us and we will review it.
