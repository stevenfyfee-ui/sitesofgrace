{% comment %} The illustrated California map. Two layers in one fixed viewBox: 1. .fs-map-art: aria-hidden: watercolor Pacific and California on the real outline (Census, public domain; projected by future_saints.geo), the neighbouring states as plain parchment land, a soft terrain tint, the dotted trail through the missions in the Mission Trail's stop order, four city ribbons in the Pacific with leader lines to each city's real position, and the compass rose and "California Mission Trail" scroll in the ocean. A painted basemap can replace the fills later (map-art-template.svg). 2. .fs-map-pins: the interactive layer. Each mission is a real submit button of a GET form (so it works without JavaScript), placed at its canonical latitude/longitude through the same projection, in founding order (= tab order). The box keeps the viewBox's aspect ratio, so percentages match the art. {% endcomment %}
